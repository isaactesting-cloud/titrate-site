Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Titrate ("the App") is developed by Isaac Abraham. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Titrate does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter — including child profiles, medications and doses, daily observations, side effects, notable events, and growth measurements — is stored locally on your device only. The developer has no access to it.

No Accounts or Registration

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it.

No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use analytics services, advertising frameworks, or any third-party tracking tools. No usage data is collected or shared.

No Network Communication

The App does not communicate with external servers. It functions entirely offline. The only network activity that may occur is through standard iOS platform services (such as checking for app updates through the App Store), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Local Notifications

If you turn them on, the App sends local notifications to remind you to log daily observations and to give scheduled doses. A dose reminder shows your child's first name and the medication name on your device. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device and do not involve any external service. You can turn them off at any time in the App's Settings.

App Lock (Face ID, Touch ID, Passcode)

You can choose to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode to open the App. This check is carried out by your device's operating system. The App only learns whether the check succeeded; it never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data or passcode.

Reports, Backups, and Files

You may choose to generate PDF reports, save a report to the Files app on your device, or export all of your data as a backup file. These files are created locally on your device and leave it only when you choose to share them. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere safe. How you share or store these files after export is under your control and not governed by this policy.

You may also restore your data from a backup file you select. The App reads only the file you choose, and its contents stay on your device.

Device Backups

Your data may be included in your device's standard backup (such as iCloud Backup), if you have that turned on. These backups are managed by your device's operating system and are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by parents and guardians, not by children. While it is designed to help parents track medication for their children, it does not collect or transmit any data about children or anyone else. All information remains on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete a child's profile, or all data, within the App at any time. Deleting all data also removes saved report files and scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the App will permanently remove all data stored by the App on your device. Files you have already shared or saved elsewhere, and device backups, are not affected.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact
